### EPIC-001 · approved · source: planning:EPIC-001

The calculator performs the four basic arithmetic operations on decimal numbers and shows the result immediately. Scientific functions and percentages are out of scope for this version.

### EPIC-002 · approved · source: planning:EPIC-002

Users enter numbers and operations using either on-screen buttons or the physical keyboard, and may enter decimal numbers. Both input methods are supported on desktop and mobile.

**Related:** US-006, US-007, US-008

### EPIC-003 · approved · source: planning:EPIC-003

Users can clear or reset the calculator at any time, and invalid operations such as division by zero show a friendly error message rather than crashing.

**Related:** US-009, US-010, US-011

### EPIC-004 · approved · source: planning:EPIC-004

The calculator is delivered as a single-page, responsive web app that works on desktop and mobile, requires no login or accounts, is free with no payments, and runs all calculations client-side with no server-side storage or persistent history.

Assumption stated: no login or accounts, no payments or subscriptions, and no saved history are in scope for this version.
